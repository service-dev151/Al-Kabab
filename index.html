Our Heritage & Story

Born from Coal, Shaped by Craft

Situated on Main Road Abbasia Town opposite the Railway Line in Rahim Yar Khan, Al-Kabab Restaurant is synonymous with authentic Pakistani barbecue. We reject shortcuts, commercial powders, and artificial coloring.

Every skewer is pressed by master ustads, seasoned with whole toasted cumin, raw papaya juice, and wild podina, then grilled over open mangrove coal. Combined with pillow-soft Roghani Naan brushed with desi butter, this is authentic hospitality.
